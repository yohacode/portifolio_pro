About Me

Building software,

I'm Yohannes Zerihun — a Full-Stack PHP Developer, IT Teacher, and STEM Instructor based in Hawassa, Ethiopia. My journey began with a Diploma in Information Technology from Mary Help College Dilla, and it's grown into a mission: helping businesses and students solve real-world problems through software, automation, and technology education.

I believe in learning by building. Whether I'm developing a Laravel application, automating Excel workflows, or teaching Python and robotics to young minds, my approach is always practical, hands-on, and results-driven.

My core values — clean code, continuous learning, innovation, and teaching with purpose — guide every project I take on. I'm passionate about making technology accessible, and I love seeing my students and clients achieve more than they thought possible.
